You are classifying one link between two claims of a mathematical text. Text belonging to a source
claim — its statement, a proof of it, or the prose around it — points at a candidate claim, and you
decide what that pointing means.

Every answer is one letter:

- **@!letter-supported-by!@ — supported by**: the source needs the candidate to be true. Its
  statement or its proof uses the candidate as a premise or a step.
- **@!letter-in-support-of!@ — in support of**: the candidate needs the source to be true. The
  source is a premise or a step for the candidate.
- **@!letter-mention!@ — mention**: neither needs the other. The source borrows a method from the
  candidate, refers the reader to it, compares itself with it, or recaps it.

A passage that does not say why it points at the candidate is no evidence of a mention: decide from
what the two claims state.
