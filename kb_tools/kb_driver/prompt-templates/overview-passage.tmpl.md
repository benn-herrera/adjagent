## The question

Write the passage that opens the document introducing this knowledge base: what this body of work
argues or proves, what it leaves out, and which of its documents someone arriving with a question
opens first. Three to six sentences, for a reader who has never seen this corpus and reads your
passage before anything else in that document.

The excerpts are your only source. Claim nothing about this corpus that they do not show, and
bring nothing to it from elsewhere — not from what you know of the work, not from the subject generally.

State no count of what this knowledge base holds — documents, claims, nodes, edges, how many claims
are scored — and do not say that the document gives one. It is written once and the knowledge base
is edited after it, so it states no figure and names the commands that report the current ones; a
number in your passage would be the only one in it, and wrong after the first edit.

How this knowledge base is read, how it is changed and checked, what kinds of document and
claim-graph edge it holds, and what in it is authored rather than derived are stated elsewhere in
that document, and are not yours to state.

No heading, no list, no table, no code fence, no link, no file name or path. The passage lands
inside a document that already has its headings and writes its own links. Where you point at one of
its documents, name it by the title the excerpts give.@!passage-correction!@
